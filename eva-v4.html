Four hero routines

Whole jobs,
handed over.

The flagship job in each stream, co-created with accounting offices. EVA runs it across every client and lifts out only the one that needs you.

01Period CloseAutomationToday a close takes days, run on spreadsheets and tacit knowledge. EVA runs the checks and flags the anomalies. The period cannot be signed off until they pass and you approve.
02PayrollAdjacentEVA checks the run before you approve it and shows only the deviations: salary changes, pension, end of probation. Six or seven employees out of fifty, not the whole list.
03AdvisoryAdvisoryThe routine that creates work you cannot do today. The hour EVA gives back becomes the hour you spend advising your clients.
