Moving Images Studio

Plausible,

We are a Copenhagen studio for motion design, 3D and animation. Agencies bring us in for campaigns, pitches and visual productions, from a train of Pepsi Max cans rushing through Nørreport to the first Deepscreen campaign in the Copenhagen Metro. The aim is always the same: make it look real enough that people stop and ask.

"Wait, did that really happen?"
Craft

Reflective cans, glass bottles, honey-like serums. We light them like a product photographer and move them like a director, so the product holds up on a metro DOOH screen and in a six-second SoMe story alike.

Process

How a frame
gets made.

Notes from the Pepsi Max "Train of Thirst" production.

01The idea

Start with why the brand needs it. Here: the arrival of something new and big.

02The shot

Filmed in 4K but moved like a phone. Erratic on purpose, then 3D-tracked through the blur.

03The scan

A section of Nørreport Station, 3D-scanned to get light and scale right.

04The light

Reflective cans punish a lazy setup. Environment and every light placed with intent.

05The frame

Plausible, not possible. Right up until someone asks if it really happened.
